Why Duo Budget

  • Both paydays, one planSet each partner's pay cycle. Duo Budget plans in fortnights and converts weekly, twice-a-month and monthly pay to match, so the numbers line up with your real paydays.
  • Shared and personal, side by sideBudget the joint bills and your own spending in one place, and save toward goals together, with each person's share shown.
  • Equal partners, with room for youNo admin and no "main" account, so you're both equals. Keep your own spending in your own lines while you plan the shared stuff together.

Bank sync is coming. We're working on optional bank connections. No dates yet, and the app works fully without it. When it arrives, you choose what to share, account by account.
