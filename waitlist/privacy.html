Duo Budget waitlist: Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

This policy explains how Duo Budget ("we", "us") handles your information when you join the Duo Budget waitlist. Duo Budget is run by Damon Hosking in Australia.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Optionally, your pay cycle and your partner's pay cycle (for example, weekly or fortnightly).
  • Basic technical details needed to run the form, such as the date and time you signed up and which version of our consent wording you agreed to.

We don't ask for your name, your bank details or any financial information on the waitlist.

Why we collect it

  • To email you about the Duo Budget launch and beta.
  • To understand which pay cycles people use, so we can plan the beta.

We won't use your information for anything else without asking you first.

Emails and your consent

We only email you if you tick the box to agree. Every email we send says it's from Duo Budget and has an unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe, we'll stop emailing you within 5 business days.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or give it to anyone else for their marketing. We use a small number of service providers to run the waitlist, and they handle your information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase, our database provider. Your data is stored in its Sydney, Australia region.
  • An email service provider (to be confirmed before we send any emails), to send our emails.

Supabase is a US company. Your data is stored in Sydney, but Supabase staff may access it from overseas to provide the service. We'll name our email provider and where it's based here before we send any emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How we keep it safe

We store your information with reputable providers, and only people working on Duo Budget can access it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

How long we keep it

We delete your waitlist details 12 months after launch, or sooner if you unsubscribe or ask us to.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link in any email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us at the address below. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • You can join without giving your pay cycles. Those fields are optional.

Questions or complaints

Email duo.budget.au@gmail.com. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let people on the waitlist know by email.
